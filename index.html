Our Ethos & Journey

About Sri Sharada High School

Traditional values, moral discipline, and 21st-century academic rigor.

25+Years of Excellence

Founded with the inspiration of Goddess Sharada, patron deity of knowledge and wisdom, Sri Sharada High School has served its community for more than two decades.

We offer education from Pre-Primary through Class 10, with English and regional-medium instruction based on recognized board standards.

Beyond textbooks, we nurture curious minds, responsible citizenship, sportsmanship, critical thinking, self-discipline, and compassion.

Academic ExcellenceStrong curriculum and board preparation.
Character & ValuesIntegrity, empathy, and responsibility.
Individual AttentionPersonal mentoring and parent updates.
Holistic ActivitiesSports, yoga, arts, music, and debate.
